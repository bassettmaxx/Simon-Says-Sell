What it is:

Simon Says Sell is an advertising studio I’m building, based in SimonsTown. The long-term goal is to manage local stores’ social media and online marketing (content, posting and brand management) as ongoing contract work.

The debut: SimonsTown Revival Festival

The Revival Festival is the studio’s foot in the door. It is a community campaign managed & advertised by Simon Says Sell. Stores participating have special deals available only on the 1st and 15th of each month.
