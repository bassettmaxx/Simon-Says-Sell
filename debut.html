The Revival Festival is the studio’s foot in the door. It is a campaign to bring more customers to SimonsTown, managed & advertised by Simon Says Sell. Stores participating have special deals available only on the 1st and 15th of each month.
The festival will serve as a stepping stone to contracted work.

Go-to-market:
  • Printed posters within private properties ( stores ) in high foot-traffic areas. Posters point to Instagram, where the details and ads live.
  • Instagram ads: R255 over 15 days (about R17 a day), estimated 12,000 to 23,000 impressions.
  • Stores are encouraged to post their festival deals on their own social media, which will help the studios discoverablity.
Stores may join the festival on a commision or subscription basis; see services

The commision package includes a threshold, whish is 10% of all sales revenue after per-store threshold, on festival days only (the 1st and 15th). The threshold is a clients average daily revenue, calcualted from three months of the store’s revenue reports.

The threshold will by default be 15% higher during “season” [ December 1st to January 31st]
Break-even: the festival subscription becomes cheaper for a store once it earns more than R10,000 above its threshold across the two festival days in a month. ( because 10% would then exceed the R1000 festival subscription )

Terms and payment

Every store on the commission package must send me their invoices on festival days ( I will then calculate my commission based on their established baseline, and send them a quote )
Payment must be settled within 2 business days after each festival day ( the 1st and 15th ) Every store goes on contract, so there are legal grounds if payment is missed. A friendly reminder goes out at the end of both festival days if the store’s invoice hasn’t already arrived.
