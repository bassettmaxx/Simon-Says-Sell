The debut: SimonsTown Revival Festival The Revival Festival is the studio’s foot in the door. It is a community campaign managed & advertised by Simon Says Sell. Stores participating have special deals available only on the 1st and 15th of each month. The festival will serve as a stepping stone to contracted work. Go-to-market: Printed posters within private properties ( stores ) in high foot-traffic areas: Cape Town city centre, the corridor from the city through Wynberg to Tokai, Fish Hoek, Noordhoek, Hout Bay and Simon’s Town itself. Posters point to Instagram, where the details and ads live. Instagram ads: R255 over 15 days (about R17 a day), estimated 12,000 to 23,000 impressions. Stores are encouraged to post their festival deals on their own social media, which also opens the door to the brand management conversation. Store packages: * Festival subscription: R1,000 / month Spotlighted in the festival ads. No revenue cut. Positioned as the stable, preferred option, with brand management as the premise behind it. Social Media Consultancy included * Festival Commission: Featured in the festival ads, but not spotlighted. 10% of all sales revenue after per-store threshold, on festival days only (the 1st and 15th). The threshold is set from three months of the store’s revenue reports, calculated by me as average daily revenue. The threshold will by default be 15% higher during “season” Break-even: the festival subscription becomes cheaper for a store once it earns more than R10,000 above its threshold across the two festival days in a month. ( because 10% would then exceed the R1000 festival subscription ) Terms and payment Every store on the commission package must send me their invoices on festival days ( I will then calculate my commission based on their established baseline, and send them a quote ) And payment must be settled within 2 business days after each festival day ( the 1st and 15th ) Every store goes on contract, so there are legal grounds if payment is missed. A friendly reminder goes out at the end of both festival days if the store’s invoice hasn’t already arrived.
